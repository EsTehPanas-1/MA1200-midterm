# MA1200 Midterm Study Deck

Complete study deck for MA1200 (Calculus and Basic Linear Algebra I), Semester A 2026-27, covering Lecture Notes L1 to L6 (up to limits).

Open `index.html` in any browser. It is one self-contained file: no internet needed, and the maths (KaTeX) is built in.

## What's inside
- Full notes for L1 to L6: coordinate geometry and conics, sets and functions, polynomials and rational functions, trigonometry, exponentials and logs, limits.
- Every question from Practice Exercises 1 to 6 (limits questions only for Ch.6), solved step by step, with graphs.
- Extra questions per chapter, Assignment 1 solutions, and "twin" questions for Assignment 2.
- A "Before the Exam" formula sheet, common-mistakes list and exam tips.

## Using it
- Use Back/Next, swipe left/right on a phone, or the left/right arrow keys; the contents list jumps to any chapter.
- Answers and solutions are hidden until you click "Show the answer and full solution". Tick "Show all solutions" to open them all, or press `S`.
- Works on phone and desktop, in light and dark mode.
